Cascade is a little ritual of order and possibility. Settle into a green felt table, turn over a fresh deal, and find a path through a classic game of Klondike solitaire. There is no timer to race: take one card at a time.

Build the columns downward, alternating red and black, and uncover the cards waiting underneath. Move an entire face-up run when you need room. Empty columns welcome Kings; the four foundations grow from Ace to King, each in its own suit.

The stock turns three cards at once. Only the front card is yours to play, so every move can open another possibility. When the stock runs out, turn the waste back over and make another pass. Drag cards with a mouse or your fingertip, or double-click an exposed card to send it home.

Bring all fifty-two cards home and the tidy table gives way to a celebration. Cards leap from the foundations, fall in bright arcs, bounce along the floor, and leave overlapping trails across the felt. When the last card drifts away, your painted table remains. Click to start another quiet game.
